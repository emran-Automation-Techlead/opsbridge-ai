# Policy Change Process

> SYNTHETIC policy text for a fictional company. Not real policy.

## Proposal

A policy change request must name the policy owner, the policy identifier, a summary of the change, and the business reason.

## Review

Product Management reviews the change for customer impact. Policy Operations reviews it for consistency with other policies.

## Approval and effective date

The policy owner and a director approve the change. A new policy takes effect no earlier than 14 days after approval so that teams can prepare.
