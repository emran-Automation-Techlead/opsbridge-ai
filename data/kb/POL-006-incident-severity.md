# Incident Severity

> SYNTHETIC policy text for a fictional company. Not real policy.

## Severity 1

A severity 1 incident is a full outage or a confirmed data exposure. The on-call engineer must respond within 15 minutes and a manager is notified at once.

## Severity 2

A severity 2 incident is a major feature degraded for many users. Response is required within 1 hour.

## Review

Every severity 1 incident requires a written review within 5 business days.
