# Content Retention Policy

> SYNTHETIC policy text for a fictional company. Not real policy.

## Retention periods

Published content is retained in the active repository for 24 months after publication. After 24 months it is moved to the archive and removed from search. Draft content that has not been updated for 90 days is deleted automatically.

## Legal hold

A legal hold overrides every retention period. Content under legal hold must not be archived or deleted until Legal Operations releases the hold in writing.

## Ownership

Each content item has one named owner. The owner is responsible for confirming that retention settings are correct at publication.
