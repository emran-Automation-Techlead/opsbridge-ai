# Content Takedown Procedure

> SYNTHETIC policy text for a fictional company. Not real policy.

## Intake

Takedown requests are logged in the ticketing system and triaged within 2 business days.

## Escalation

Requests that cite copyright, defamation or a court order are high risk and must be escalated to Legal Operations on the same day. Content is not removed before Legal Operations confirms.

## Records

Every takedown decision is recorded with the requester, the reason, the decision, and the approver.
