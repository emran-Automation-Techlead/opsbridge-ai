# Vendor Onboarding

> SYNTHETIC policy text for a fictional company. Not real policy.

## Requirements

Every new vendor must complete the security questionnaire before any data is shared. Vendors that process personal data must also sign a data processing agreement.

## Review time

Security reviews of standard vendors take 10 business days. Critical vendors are reviewed within 20 business days.

## Renewal

Vendor approvals are renewed every 12 months.
